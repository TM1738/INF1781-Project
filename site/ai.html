3. AI and decision support systems

A decision support system (DSS) helps a person decide; it does not decide for them. AI means software that finds patterns in data to predict or suggest. For a small retailer this is already practical and often cheap.

Useful suggestions

ToolWhat it doesExample
Demand forecastingPredicts what will sellSuggests ordering extra braai wood before long weekends
Reorder alertsWarns before stock-outs“Maize meal: 3 days left”
ChatbotsAnswer common questionsTrading hours on WhatsApp
Generative AI assistantsDraft text and summariesWriting product descriptions

Limits and risks

  • Needs good data. A forecast built on 3 months of messy records is a guess (see information quality).
  • Bias and errors. AI can be confidently wrong. Check important outputs.
  • Privacy. Never paste customer details into public AI tools; POPIA still applies.
  • Cost versus benefit. If a simple spreadsheet rule solves it, you do not need AI.
Rule of thumb: let AI recommend, let a human approve, especially for money, staff and customer decisions.

What to do

  • Switch on low-stock alerts in your system.
  • Write a one-line staff rule for using AI tools safely.

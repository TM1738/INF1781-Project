SME Digital Transformation for Retail and Small Business

Who is this guide for?

For those who run, or work in, a small South African shop. As Stock sits in spreadsheets, customer details are in a notebook, and the books are done by hand. It worked fine until today, but it will show its limitations as you grow as a enterprise. This guide explains, in simple language, how information systems (IS) can help, and what they cost you in money, time and risk.

Key terms To Note

Data is raw facts (“12, Rooibos, R35”). Information is data that has been organised so it means something (“We sold 12 boxes of Rooibos at R35 this week”). An information system is the people, processes, data and technology that turn one into the other.
